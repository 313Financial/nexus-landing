FAQ

Common questions.

What makes Nexus different from HubSpot or Salesforce?+
Nexus is built specifically for commercial finance, not adapted from a generic CRM. It understands BTL, bridging, development finance, DIP stages, LTV and lender workflows out of the box. You don't need to configure anything. It works the way you already work.
Does it integrate with Twenty7Tec?+
Yes. Push clients and deals from Nexus directly into Twenty7Tec as cases, no rekeying between systems. Requires API access from your T7T account manager.
How does the Companies House integration work?+
When a client submits their intake form with their limited company name, Nexus automatically queries the Companies House API and pulls their registered address, company number, incorporation date, SIC codes, directors list and company status, all prepopulated into their client record before you even open it.
Can multiple advisers use it, and can I see their activity?+
Yes. The Team plan supports up to 5 advisers. As the principal, you get a master broker view. You can see all clients, deals, tasks and activity across your whole firm, or filter by any individual adviser. Everyone in your firm works from the same shared client book.
Does Nexus handle business loans?+
Yes. Business loans run through the same pipeline as your property cases. Drop the client's accounts onto the case and Nexus reads them, then works out turnover, profit and EBITDA for each year so you can see what the business can afford and present it to the lender.
Can clients sign documents electronically?+
Yes, with NexusSign, an optional add on at £4.99 a month with unlimited documents. Send a document from the case, the client verifies with a one time email code and signs on any device, and the signed copy and audit trail are saved back to the case automatically. Signing emails carry your own firm's branding.
Is my data secure?+
Yes. Nexus is hosted on enterprise grade infrastructure with row level security. Each firm's data is completely isolated from every other firm's. All data is stored in UK/EU data centres and encrypted at rest and in transit.
